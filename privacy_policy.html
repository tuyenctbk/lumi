Lumi Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 23, 2026

1. Introduction & Our Commitment

Welcome to Lumi ("we", "our", "us"). We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy of our young users and their families. Because Lumi is designed primarily for children under the age of 13, we adhere to the strictest standards of safety, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K).

✨ Lumi does NOT collect, store, or share any personal information. Your child's privacy and safety are our absolute top priority.

2. Information Collection & Processing

Lumi operates strictly offline-first. We do not require account registration, login credentials, or personal profiles. Here is how we handle specific permissions and hardware:

  • Microphone Voice Data: To enable interactive pronunciation activities, we request permission to access the device's microphone. All audio analysis and recognition are processed 100% locally on the device in real-time. Audio recordings are never saved, stored, or transmitted to any servers.
  • Push Notifications: We request notification access solely to suggest timely parent-approved physical screen-break movement quests. No data is shared or transmitted to trigger these local scheduling systems.
  • Local Database Persistence (Room): Any progress tracking (learned words, retention scores, sticker collections, and current streak statistics) is saved exclusively on the local storage of your child's physical device and never uploaded to the cloud.

3. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

We strictly comply with COPPA regulations. We do not intentionally or knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 years of age.

If you believe that we have accidentally or inadvertently collected any such information, please contact us immediately so we can promptly delete it from any local cache or logs.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

To provide a completely safe and uninterrupted learning experience for children:

  • We do not display third-party advertisements.
  • We do not include data brokers, trackers, or marketing SDKs.
  • We do not use any user profiling or behavior-tracking technologies.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our app or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage parents to review this policy regularly to stay informed about our child-safe guidelines.
